About

Football Tactics explains the game's key patterns in a simple way. Every tactic is drawn with dots and arrows and comes with a short caption.

Legend: blue dots = your team, red dots = opponents, solid arrows = passes, dashed arrows = off-the-ball runs.

FAQ

Do the tactics work with any formation?

They are basic patterns that adapt to any formation and age group.

What do the dots and arrows mean?

Blue dots: your team. Red: opponents. Solid arrow: pass. Dashed arrow: run without the ball.

Can I use the diagrams with my team?

Of course, they are made for training sessions and tactics boards.

How do I zoom into a tactic?

Click any diagram to open it in a larger view, then use the zoom buttons or the arrow keys to browse.
